Remembering Ken Steif: A Leader in Urban Analytics (UPenn)"

Prof. Ken Steif, the Director of University of Pennsylvania's Weitzman School of Design, was the person who inspired me to begin my journey in Urban Spatial Science. I was admitted to the MSc program at UPenn, but before I could meet my mentor, he sadly passed away. I eventually chose to continue my spatial science adventure at UCL, carrying forward the spirit of his work.
